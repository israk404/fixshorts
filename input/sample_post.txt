Title: Laptop Wi-Fi disconnects every 10-15 minutes, have to turn Wi-Fi off and on to get it back (Windows 11)

Lenovo IdeaPad, Windows 11. Every 10 to 15 minutes the Wi-Fi just drops. The icon shows no internet and I have to toggle Wi-Fi off and on. My phone and my brother's laptop on the same router are totally fine, so it's not the router. Already restarted everything. Any ideas?

Top comments:
[412 upvotes] Device Manager > Network adapters > your wifi card > Properties > Power Management tab > uncheck "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power". Windows keeps putting the card to sleep. Fixed mine instantly.
  [OP reply] This helped a lot, it went from every 10 min to maybe once a day.
[230 upvotes] Don't use the driver Windows Update installs. Get the newest one straight from Intel or Realtek (whichever card you have) or from Lenovo's support page.
  [OP reply] Updated the Intel driver from Intel's site and it hasn't dropped once since yesterday. Thank you!
[96 upvotes] If nothing else works: Settings > Network & internet > Advanced network settings > Network reset. Heads up it forgets all your saved wifi passwords.
[60 upvotes] Whatever you do, don't install those "driver booster" or "driver updater" apps. Half of them bundle junk and they install the wrong drivers.
[40 upvotes] just buy a new laptop lol
[18 upvotes] run netsh winsock reset in cmd, worked for me once on windows 7
